# Privacy Policy

Complete privacy is the only option.

Because samatā.ml runs entirely in your browser, your data **never leaves your machine**. All data processing, model training, and evaluation happen locally. This web app does not collect, store, or transmit your datas or models to any external servers. The public beta hosted at `beta.samata.ml` uses minimal cookieless analytics as described below.

### Beta analytics and error reporting

Accepting cookieless analytics and error reporting by clicking **Get Started** is a condition of using the public beta at `beta.samata.ml`.

- **Provider and scope:** The beta uses **Umami Cloud**, a cookieless analytics service, on `beta.samata.ml` only. Outside the beta hostname and in the stable V1 release, nothing is collected or sent.
- **What the app sends:** Only canonical workflow page paths (`/`, `/data`, `/target`, `/models`, `/results`), your **Get Started** acceptance event, whether a dataset was loaded from a sample or a local upload (category only), training start/completion/failure milestones, fixed error category names (such as a worker crash or unhandled error), screen dimensions, and browser language.
- **What Umami Cloud receives:** With every analytics request, Umami Cloud receives your IP address and browser user agent, from which it derives approximate location, browser, operating system, and device type without setting cookies.
- **What is never collected:** Your CSV datasets, filenames, row counts, column names, cell values, target names, model configurations, model results, error messages, stack traces, cookies, referrers, or user identifiers never leave your browser.
