## How Will AI Shape Our Future?

Over the next decades as the excitement about AI calms algorithms will become an increasing part of everyday life. For this to be a bright future, we need more people with expertise in charge of the algorithms.
**This is the core mission of samatā.ml**.

### My name is [Alex Duncan](https://alexduncan.co.uk/) and during my masters in AI & Ethics I learned:

- Machines learn from historical data.
- Historical data inevitably includes bias that we don't want machines to perpetuate.
- Current AI courses require a high level of technical knowledge that isn't intrinsic to understanding how algorithms are biased.\
  _(Some research shows that the code can even get in the way.)_

For society to have safer algorithms we need more people who understand AI. To achieve this we need tools to help learners who don't have deep knowlege of maths and coding. **samatā.ml** is my attempt to build that tool.

## Current Status

This version of **samatā.ml** is a prototype I built for my masters disseration. It is a proof of concept with a few basic models and datasets which you can configure and train without writing a single line of code. I built this first version following X key principles:

1. **Privacy**\
   The whole app runs completely inside your web browser. No data is sent to a remote server.

2. **Experimentation**\
   Research shows that people learn better by trying different things and learning from the results. samatā.ml lets you train multiple models using different settings and see how they compare.

3. **Control**
   Nothing is automatic – you make all the choices.

4. **Accessibility**\
   Huge effort has been put into making the app user-friendly and providing guidance written in non-technical language to help you understand complex terms.

There is so much work needed to improve **samatā.ml** so please send feedback and get involved.
