Privacy Policy

Because samatā.ml runs entirely in your browser, your data never leaves your machine. All data processing, model training and evaluation happen locally.

What is stored

  • Your session state, in localStorage.
  • The dataset you load, in IndexedDB on this device.

Clearing your data

  1. Open your browser's site settings.
  2. Choose Clear data for this site.

We don't even track page views.

How local computation works

All CSV parsing, column type detection, summary statistics, stratified data splitting, and classifier training run inside isolated browser Web Workers on your computer. No training files or predictions are transmitted to a server.

Each trained model stores its evaluation metrics and confusion counts locally so you can inspect calibration, threshold trade-offs, and cross-validation folds without reloading your file.

If you choose to switch datasets, clearing the active dataset removes the stored rows and derived models from local browser storage immediately.
